At a glance

StrideBoard is a step-count game for students and staff at {{ school }}, built by a student team. It collects only what the game needs. Nothing it collects is sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything except running StrideBoard.

  • Steps, not tracking. StrideBoard stores a daily step total. It never uses GPS, location, weight, BMI, photos, contacts or the microphone.
  • Small amounts of optional data. Water glasses, a daily mood (1 to 5) and activities are only stored if you choose to log them.
  • Emails are not kept. An email address is only used to send a sign-up or reset code, then deleted within a day. Your account keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of it.
  • Students and teachers are kept apart. In the app, students only see student boards and teachers only see teacher boards.
  • No ads and no tracking cookies. The only cookies keep you logged in and remember your display settings.
  • You can ask to be removed. An admin can delete an account and everything linked to it.

What we never collect

  • Location or GPS, routes or where anyone walked
  • Weight, height, BMI, heart rate, sleep or any other health readings
  • Photos, videos, contacts, messages or microphone audio
  • Date of birth, home address, phone number or Emirates ID
  • Advertising IDs, or anything from other apps

On phones, StrideBoard only asks Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone) for the step count. It does not request any other kind of health data.

Where data is stored and how it is protected

StrideBoard runs on PythonAnywhere, a web hosting service whose servers are outside the UAE. All data is in one database on that server.

  • Encrypted connection. The site is served over HTTPS, so data is encrypted between your device and the server.
  • Protected logins. PINs and email fingerprints are stored only in one-way scrambled form. Wrong PIN attempts are limited, and sign-up and reset codes expire after 15 minutes.
  • Cookies. Three small cookies: one keeps you logged in, and two remember your theme and Fast mode. Scripts can’t read them, and there are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Phones. The Android app keeps steps on the phone only until it can send them (for example after a trip with no signal). The iPhone Shortcut sends the day’s step total and nothing else.
  • Other services. Gmail sends sign-up and PIN reset codes. The Weekly Wrap-Up chart loads a drawing library (Chart.js) from jsDelivr, which sees your internet address but no StrideBoard data. No other outside service receives anything.

Terms of use

By creating an account, everyone agrees to these rules.

  1. Use your real name. One account per person, in your real class or department. Moderators may change names that aren’t real or appropriate, and you’ll be told when they do.
  2. Play fair. Only count steps you really walked. Shaking a phone, using machines or apps that fake steps, or typing made-up numbers is cheating. Totals over 100,000 steps a day are refused.
  3. Keep it kind. Names, avatars and anything else you add must be respectful. No bullying, teasing or pressuring anyone about their steps.
  4. Keep your PIN and codes private. Don’t share your PIN. Staff shouldn’t share the big-screen code outside school.
  5. Moderation. Admins can time out, ban or delete accounts that break these rules. A banned email can be blocked from signing up again.
  6. The service. StrideBoard is a free school project, provided as it is. We work to keep it running and accurate, but it may sometimes be offline, and step counts depend on each phone.
  7. Ownership. The StrideBoard app and code belong to its creators, {{ team }}. Data in StrideBoard belongs to the people who entered it and is managed for {{ school }}.

Health, changes and contact

Not medical advice. Step goals, reminders and the Weekly Wrap-Up are general suggestions to move more. Anyone with a health condition or injury should follow their doctor’s or the school nurse’s guidance.

Changes. If StrideBoard starts collecting anything new, this page is updated first and users are told on the Home page. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions or requests. For a copy of your data, a correction, deletion or any privacy question, contact {{ contact }}.
